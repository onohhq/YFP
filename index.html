What happens at the booth

A Photo Experience is a small portrait studio we build inside your venue. It fits in a space of about 8 by 8 to 10 by 10 feet and turns one corner of the room into the spot every guest visits.

  • We set up the lighting and backdrop one to two hours before guests arrive.
  • On the Portrait Booth, a live photographer directs every pose.
  • Guests get prints or digital copies in minutes, depending on the experience.
  • You receive a gallery of the night to keep and share.
